Mon Métier

A web application designed to manage the informal sector, connecting foremen with workers, employers with job seekers or apprentices. It allows users to share their experiences and more with each other.

Project awarded 3rd place at the SmartCo Benin competition 2019.

  • Technologies : Python/Django, Node.js, PostgreSQL, OSM
  • Rôle : Lead developer
  • Year : 2019
